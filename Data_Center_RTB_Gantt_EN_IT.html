Parallel tenant, client and exit track

The tenant is the end user of capacity. The client/buyer is the party acquiring the RTB project or contracting the build. Tenant engagement begins once site control and a credible power path exist; it should remain flexible until the technical envelope is sufficiently defined. A tenant LOI can increase value but is not mandatory for base RTB.
